# Launch pricing

Monthly, in INR, **excluding 18% GST** (GST handling is a founder decision). Seeded by `supabase/seed/plans.sql`. AI provider and Meta conversation fees are billed to the customer directly, so they are not in these prices.

| Plan | Price | Numbers | Members | Contacts | AI agents | AI replies/mo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | ₹999 | 1 | 3 | 1,000 | 1 | 500 |
| Growth | ₹2,499 | 1 | 10 | 10,000 | 3 | 3,000 |
| Pro | ₹5,999 | 3 | 25 | 50,000 | 10 | 15,000 |

## Market check (July–Nov 2026, third-party roundups, verify on vendor pages)
- AiSensy entry ≈ ₹1,500/month; WATI Growth ≈ ₹2,699–2,999, Pro ≈ ₹5,799–6,499; Interakt Growth ≈ ₹2,799, higher plan ≈ ₹6,999. Template message fees are extra on all of them.
- Sources: [codingclave WATI](https://codingclave.com/blog/wati-pricing-india-2026), [codingclave Interakt](https://codingclave.com/blog/interakt-pricing-india-2026), [codingclave AiSensy](https://codingclave.com/blog/aisensy-pricing-india-2026), [go4whatsup comparison](https://go4whatsup.com/blog/aisensy-vs-wati-vs-interakt-pricing-showdown-2026/).

## Why these prices
New, unproven product, so it sits about 10–15% under WATI and Interakt at Growth and Pro, and under AiSensy at Starter. Variable cost per customer is small (the customer's own AI and Meta keys pay for usage), so margin is driven by fixed hosting of roughly US$45/month, covered by about 6 Starter customers.

## Not proven
There is no buyer panel behind these numbers. Treat them as a starting test: offer a launch price to the first customers and watch conversion at Growth before changing anything. Plans can be edited in the operator console.
